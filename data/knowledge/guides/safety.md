# Safety guide

## Battery management system (BMS)
A BMS protects against overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent, short circuit and over- or under-temperature, and balances cells. Packs without a BMS (common in hobby drone packs) need a balance charger and careful supervision, and should not be left charging unattended.

## Charging
- Use the charger supplied or specified by the manufacturer.
- Do not charge below 0°C (LiFePO4 and Li-ion) or above about 45°C.
- Do not charge a swollen, dented or leaking pack. Take it out of service.

## Heat in Melbourne
Summer days can exceed 40°C. Never leave lithium packs in a parked vehicle or in direct sun. Keep them in the shade and let a hot pack cool before charging.

## Storage
Store lithium packs at roughly 40-60% charge in a cool, dry place away from flammable material. Avoid storing fully charged or fully flat for long periods.

## Installation
Fixed battery systems connected to inverters in Australia fall under AS/NZS 5139 (safety of battery systems for use with power conversion equipment). Have a licensed electrician install them.

## Disposal
Do not put batteries in household bins or kerbside recycling. Use a battery recycling drop-off point, for example a B-cycle collection point for smaller batteries (check size limits), or a council hazardous waste service.
